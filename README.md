# Mr. Bill's Plumbing — Website Case Study

**Live site:** [mrbillsplumbing.grok.me](https://mrbillsplumbing.grok.me)

A conversion-focused local service website for a family plumbing shop in Cleburne, TX (est. 1993). Built as a portfolio piece for **web design** and **local digital marketing** — clear offer, trust signals, and a path to call or request a quote.

---

## The brief

Local homeowners and businesses in Johnson, Hood, and Tarrant counties need a plumber they trust. The site had to:

- Rank and convert for local intent (Cleburne + nearby cities)
- Feel like a real family shop — not a franchise call center
- Make the next step obvious: call or request a quote
- Surface license, service area, reviews, and emergency availability

---

## What shipped

| Area | Approach |
|------|----------|
| **Positioning** | "The plumber Cleburne has called since 1993" — local, full-time crew, free estimates |
| **Services** | Repairs, leak detection, water heaters, drains/sewer, commercial |
| **Trust** | RMP 32141 on the page, neighbor reviews, "not a franchise" story |
| **Conversion** | Phone CTA + quote request repeated through the page |
| **Local SEO** | City/service-area coverage, FAQ block for common search questions |
| **After hours** | Emergency messaging with rate transparency |
